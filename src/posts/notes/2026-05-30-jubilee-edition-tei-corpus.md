Almost every Russian word on this site comes, at one or two removes, from two artifacts. This dive documents both.

The first is the **Jubilee Edition** — Tolstoy's *Полное собрание сочинений*, 90 volumes published 1928–1958, plus a 1964 index volume. It was a Soviet-state project (authorised by a Sovnarkom resolution of 23 June 1925, around the 1828→1928 centenary), and the government entrusted the editing to the friend Tolstoy himself had chosen: **V. G. Chertkov**, his disciple and the keeper of his manuscripts. Its founding principle was maximalist — reproduce *absolutely everything* Tolstoy wrote — and Chertkov's foreword roots the whole edition in Tolstoy's testamentary request of 31 July 1910, the same document behind [the copyright-renunciation dive](https://github.com/tolstoylife/tolstoy.life/blob/main/docs/research/themes/copyright-renunciation/index.md). The edition is honest about its Soviet-era limits, too: its own front matter reserves a right of omission. And — fittingly for a writer who gave his copyright away — Tom 1 carries a printed free-reproduction notice: «Перепечатка разрешается безвозмездно.»

The second is the **tolstoydigital TEI/XML corpus**. The chain from print to data runs: scans from the Russian State Library → a 2013–14 crowdsourcing project, «Весь Толстой в один клик» (started by Fyokla Tolstaya with the Tolstoy Museum and ABBYY; ~3,249 volunteers in 49 countries proofread ~46,820 pages) → free e-texts on tolstoy.ru → TEI/XML encoding with a semantic layer by the **Tolstoy Digital / «Слово Толстого»** group at HSE (2015–2022), under CC BY-SA.

Locally that corpus is 16,573 TEI files (works, letters, diaries, notebooks, *Круг чтения*, *Азбука*, commentary), plus a reference layer of 3,113 persons, 770 places, a works catalogue, a 125-category taxonomy, a rare-word dictionary, and a museum-photo index. Each person even carries a Wikidata id. That reference layer is what already feeds this site's wiki and works pages — so this dive doubles as a map of the people and institutions behind the edition that are themselves candidates for wiki articles.

Full reference document, evidence ledger, and entity work-order: [The Jubilee Edition and the tolstoydigital TEI/XML corpus](https://github.com/tolstoylife/tolstoy.life/blob/main/docs/research/_meta/jubilee-edition-tei-corpus/index.md).
