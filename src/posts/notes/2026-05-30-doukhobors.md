The Doukhobor affair is the largest piece of *practical* moral action in Tolstoy's life — the point where his doctrine of non-resistance stopped being a body of writing and became the organised rescue of several thousand people.

A survey of where that conversation lives in the corpus has sat in the research tree since May; this week it was re-run as a structured corpus-dive across the 90-volume Jubilee Edition and the tolstoydigital TEI corpus. The sweep on the stem *духобор* returns 538 letters, 28 diary entries, 22 published works and 34 commentary files — and the spine of the story is all in Tolstoy's own words: the Burning of Arms of June 1895, the open letter to the foreign press, the afterwords to Biryukov's article and to the appeal «Help!», the article *Two Wars*, the two petitions to Nicholas II, and the long doctrinal rebuke he sent the community once it was safe in Canada.

Three things the re-run added:

- **A foregrounded account of how the state handled it.** Tolstoy's writing is the fullest contemporary catalogue of the means used against the Doukhobors — the Cossack charge and the mock execution at Kars, the systematic punishment of families, and his own four demands to the Tsar on the law of religious persecution.
- **A visual record.** Photographs and engravings — the Caucasus villages, Verigin and the relief circle, the steamers and the Cyprus camp, the landing at Grosse Île, and the prairie settlement — were gathered from Wikimedia Commons, Library and Archives Canada, and the Doukhobor heritage record, each with its rights logged.
- **A machine-readable dossier** that maps the evidence, the people and works it should feed into the wiki, and where the primary record confirms, complicates or extends the conventional scholarship.

One correction the primary record makes to the popular story: *Resurrection* money mattered, but it was one of three comparable funding streams (alongside the English Quakers and the Doukhobors' own savings), not the whole of it — and Tolstoy himself later doubted whether money had been the right instrument at all.

The full survey, with byte-faithful Russian quotations and working English translations, is in [`docs/research/doukhobors/`](https://github.com/tolstoylife/tolstoy.life/blob/main/docs/research/doukhobors/index.md) (`index.md` and `dossier.yaml`).
