In 1908, his eightieth year, Tolstoy wrote what would be his last systematic statement of non-resistance: *Закон насилия и закон любви* — **The Law of Violence and the Law of Love**. This corpus dive reads the treatise from its own text in the Jubilee Edition (PSS Tom 37), reconstructs how it was written from the 1908 diaries and letters, and follows it into print.

A few things stood out.

**It is the end of a line.** The argument runs from *What I Believe* (1884) through *The Kingdom of God Is Within You* (1893) to this treatise. Its single hinge: non-resistance to evil by force is not a separate rule but the strict, no-exceptions consequence of love being the *highest* law. A Christianity that allows violent exceptions, Tolstoy writes, "is the same internal contradiction as cold fire or hot ice."

**It blames revolution and government equally.** Written after the 1905 revolution and its repression, the treatise condemns state violence and revolutionary violence in the same breath — the revolutionaries "put out fire with fire," and both sides are like men who "break down the walls of the house they live in and burn them."

**The unstable title is the story of its making.** It began as "On Decline, Unbelief and Non-resistance," was first titled "Everything Comes to an End," and only became "The Law of Violence and the Law of Love" on 12 May 1908. Along the way Tolstoy reworked the newspaper chapter nine times and then split it off into a separate article, folded a 1906 essay in as a chapter, and demoted three chapters to appendices. His diary records him nearly abandoning it — "it won't go, yet I don't want to leave it" — and his secretary caught him calling it "nonsense," before, on 12 May, the turn: "I liked it, and I have finished it."

**Unlike most of the late work, it appeared in Russia — censored.** Its first printing was a partial, censored run in a Kyiv newspaper in February 1909; the complete text reached the public only abroad, through Chertkov's Free Age Press.

The dive produced a cited [research write-up](../../../docs/research/works/non-fiction/treatises/1908-the-law-of-violence-and-the-law-of-love/index.html) and a machine-readable dossier, and — because this treatise has no record in the works database yet — a proposal to create one. As always, the dive plans the vault pages; it does not write them.
