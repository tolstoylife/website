This Notes section is a dated record of the research, design, and engineering decisions behind tolstoy.life.

It is not documentation. Documentation lives elsewhere — in the wiki, in the source repos, in the schemas. Notes are *snapshots in time*: what was tried, what was decided, what surprised us, what we learned. They are honest about the state of the work, and explicit about when they were written.

The first entries are imported from a build log that lived in a separate `docs/` directory during the research phase. Older entries are backdated to when they were originally written.
