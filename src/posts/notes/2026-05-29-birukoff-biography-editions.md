## The question

Paul Birukoff (1860–1931) compiled the only contemporaneous, Tolstoy-authorised biography of Leo Tolstoy. Four Russian volumes (1906, 1908, 1915, 1922), at least four translations (Swedish, German, English, Dutch), one English-language separate volume that Birukoff supplemented for the British market (Cassell 1911). The project's existing Vol III and Vol IV English translations were sourced from `tolstoy-lit.ru` and later `az.lib.ru` — both of which trace back to a 2000 «Алгоритм» Moscow reprint. The chain ends at a 21st-century edition, two generations removed from Birukoff's hand.

Before extending the project's work to Vols I and II ([TODO §9](../../../TODO.md)) or backfilling Vol III's ~4.5% material gap (TODO §8), one question needed answering:

> Which edition of Birukoff's biography should the project's citations and translations be grounded in, and why?

## Method

Four sessions across three days. Direct catalogue probing (az.lib.ru, Russian Wikipedia, Egorov auction lots, contemporary émigré reviews, German national-library records, Swedish digital archives, an antiquarian publisher's archive). No translation work, no critical-theory framing — the deliverable is an evidence base for an editorial decision, not a literary essay.

The single most valuable surface was **Mark Aldanov's 1921 review of the Berlin Ladyzhnikov reissue** in *Современные записки*, Book VIII — six kB of émigré literary criticism that confirms (a) the Berlin 1921 reissue adds one previously-suppressed chapter on Tolstoy's pre-marriage romance with a society lady whose surname Birukoff declined to publish; (b) otherwise Berlin 1921 = first edition unchanged; (c) Vol IV had not yet appeared in 1921, locating it firmly in 1922. Full transcription + working English at [`extracts/aldanov-1921-review-berlin-ladyzhnikov.md`](../../research/biryukov-biography-editions/extracts/aldanov-1921-review-berlin-ladyzhnikov.md).

The second most valuable surface was the **Vol III front matter on az.lib.ru** — a two-layer preface, Layer 1 written from С. Ивановское on 24 July 1909 with Tolstoy alive and the work conceived as three volumes; Layer 2 written from Onex près Genève on 1 November 1915 with Tolstoy dead, recording Birukoff's explicit decision to expand to four volumes with *Resurrection* (1899) as the Vol III/IV boundary. The famous "raw form" admission — that the Vol III material "is presented to the reader in almost raw form, I had not the strength for a more thorough working-through of it, let others do this" — is in Layer 2, applies specifically to Vol III, and is editorial rather than evidentiary. Transcription at [`extracts/az-lib-vol3-front-matter.md`](../../research/biryukov-biography-editions/extracts/az-lib-vol3-front-matter.md).

The third was the **Swedish edition Slutord, p. 453** — physically held in Johan's possession, photographed and transcribed. Birukoff's closing words to the Swedish Andra delen (Kostroma, byn Ivanskoje, 27 August 1908) forward-flag the Vol III plan with Tolstoy still alive: *"For the following, third part of the biography, my own personal memories will also serve as the basis... Owing to these circumstances, Part III will, from a literary standpoint, have a somewhat divergent character."* The 1915 "raw form" admission therefore reads as a confession of *editorial incompleteness*, not of *evidentiary weakness* — Vol III's memoir register was Birukoff's published design from 1908, not a symptom of exile.

## The verdict

Settled per volume, signed off 2026-05-29:

| Volume | Source-of-record | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **Vol III** | `az.lib.ru` `text_1905_tolstoy05.shtml` (= 2000 Алгоритм reprint of 1915 first edition) | **Authoritative.** 1915 was Birukoff's last word; Berlin 1921 did not revise Vol III; Birukoff died 1931 with no later Vol III revision known |
| **Vol IV** | `az.lib.ru` `text_1922_tolstoy06.shtml` (= 2000 Алгоритм reprint of 1922 Berlin first edition) | **Authoritative.** 1922 Berlin is the only authorial edition; nine years to Birukoff's 1931 death, no revision known |
| **Vols I–II** | `az.lib.ru` `text_1905_tolstoy01.shtml`–`04.shtml` (= 2000 Алгоритм reprint, contains the 99 kB chapter "Глава 10. Роман" with full Arsenyeva correspondence regardless of source-edition) | **Working source.** Aldanov's residual claim — that Berlin 1921 added a chapter on a *different* society lady — is flagged as a known open question, settleable by a future Berlin 1921 diff |
| **Swedish (Norstedt 1906–1909)** | Johan's three physical books + iPhone photographs | **Cross-check, not replacement.** Independent Tolstoy-reviewed witness for Vols I–II from before the 1919 suppression lifted |

Verdict full text and reasoning at [§5 of the reference document](../../research/biryukov-biography-editions/index.md#5-preferred-edition-verdict).

## Side-findings

Three findings showed up that weren't on the search plan.

**The Vol III preface is a two-layer document.** Layer 1 in 1909 with Tolstoy alive, three-volume plan. Layer 2 in 1915 with Tolstoy dead, four-volume plan, "raw form" admission. The two-layer structure rehabilitates Vol III: the "raw form" admission is editorial-incompleteness, not evidentiary-weakness, because Vol III's memoir register was Birukoff's published design from 1908 (Swedish Slutord), not a symptom of Geneva exile. Aldanov's qualified verdict on Vol III ("I would not say [one cannot tear oneself away from] the third book") is consistent with the work still being authoritative — a *different kind* of biographical writing, not a worse one.

**The 2000 «Алгоритм» reprint is split into Book I (Russian Vols I+II) and Book II (Russian Vols III+IV).** The az.lib.ru file numbering `tolstoy01`–`tolstoy06` maps cleanly to the physical source. The same OCR'er (Vitaly Adamenko) digitised both books between October 2002 and January 2003, both deposited on az.lib.ru. A **second independent electronic edition** of the same Алгоритм 2000 print exists at [imwerden.de/publ-16339](https://imwerden.de/publ-16339), prepared by Andrey Nikitin-Perensky and Stanislav Lvovskiy. Either can serve as an OCR-noise cross-check on the other.

**The Cassell 1911 English single volume is not just a translation.** Per the Cassell Publishers' Note: *"Tolstoy himself actually collaborated with him [Birukoff] in the present work"* and Birukoff supplied a prefatory note and an account of Tolstoy's final days **specifically for the English edition**. Cassell 1911 is therefore simultaneously a translation, a 226-page condensation, and an original authorial supplement — the English-specific prefatory and final-days material exists nowhere else. The translator is unidentified in the Internet Archive metadata; the conventional Aylmer-Maude attribution is a hypothesis still to verify against the title page.

## Two previously-unknown Russian editions surfaced at session 4

The 2026-05-29 catalogue probe (transcript at [`extracts/digital-surrogate-hunt-2026-05-29.md`](../../research/biryukov-biography-editions/extracts/digital-surrogate-hunt-2026-05-29.md)) located all three Berlin 1921 Ladyzhnikov volumes as OCR'd PDFs at [vtoraya-literatura.com/author-168](https://vtoraya-literatura.com/author-168) — Vol I 576 pp, Vol II 680 pp, Vol III 616 pp, total ~109 MB. Page counts match Egorov's lot 192 photographs (572 + 675 + 611 pp) ±5 pp.

Along the way, two editions that the project did not know existed surfaced:

- A **1911 Moscow Кушнерёв second edition of Vol I** at [imwerden.de/publ-18037](https://imwerden.de/publ-18037), 544 pp, OCR PDF. Between the 1906/1908 first edition and the 1921 Berlin reissue. Crucially: 1911 is pre-S. A. Tolstaya death (1919), so the suppression layer Aldanov names still applies.
- A **1923 Moscow + Petrograd Soviet state publishing house (Госиздат) third edition Vol I**, «Издание 3-е, исправленное и дополненное», XX + 243 pp + 18 plates, catalogued at the [Russian Presidential Library](https://www.prlib.ru/item/1163574); physical at Ural Federal University. The 243-page count is anomalously small for a full Vol I (vs. 576 pp Berlin 1921) — likely partial-scope or split-volume. This is also probably the referent of a rumoured "1923 Berlin reissue" we'd flagged in §1.8 as one open search-hit; the Berlin attribution looks misplaced.

The 1911 Кушнерёв edition opens a clean three-way probe of the Aldanov reconciliation. Aldanov reported Berlin 1921 added a chapter on a society lady whose surname Birukoff withheld. The az.lib.ru / 2000 Алгоритм text already contains a chapter on Tolstoy's romance with Valeria Arsenyeva, named in full. Three possible reconciliations: Aldanov misremembered; or the 2000 reprint descends from a later name-restored edition; or Aldanov's "new chapter" was about a *different* woman (Tyutcheva, Behrs, or another) and the Arsenyeva chapter was always there. With the 1911 Vol I in hand, the probe becomes mechanical: does Глава 10 already exist in 1911? In what form? Single next-session executable.

## Phase 7 — the Swedish digital surrogate

Johan owns three physical Swedish books (Norstedt's *Leo Tolstoj — Hans lif och hans verk*, translated by Erik Nordenström, 1906–1909). The translator-of-record is named on the title page, the Tolstoy-review claim — *"granskade af Leo Tolstoj"* — is on the title page, the publishing house is one of Sweden's oldest (Norstedt, founded 1823, Royal Court printer). The witness value is functional: the Swedish translates the first-edition Russian Vols I–II at the moment of their publication, before Tolstoy's death and well before the 1919 S. A. Tolstaya suppression lifted.

The 2026-05-29 probe checked Project Runeberg, Litteraturbanken, Libris/KB digital collections, HathiTrust, archive.org, and the Swedish second-hand antiquarian aggregators (Bokbörsen, hstrom.se). **No digital surrogate exists at any of the standard archives.** Phase 7 (Swedish cross-check pass) stays parked on Johan's physical books — or on a partial-scope V850 scan of the post-1880 chapters of Andra delen (~8–10 h end-to-end) if Scenario A/B from [§6.2](../../research/biryukov-biography-editions/index.md#62-the-swedish-editions-specific-position) becomes a real downstream question.

The full V850 capture (the originally-planned route) was deferred at session 3 — what looked like a 3-hour scanner-active session re-costed at 30–40 hours end-to-end once Photoshop deskew, spread-to-singles splitting, Tesseract OCR with pre-1906-orthography correction, and file organisation were all priced in. The 51 iPhone photographs Johan took at session 2 cover spot-checks; they don't cover a passage-level Swedish ↔ Russian diff at Vol II's full length.

## Footnoting standard adopted for the project's English

Four types, to be standard in the project's `translation-notes.md` ledgers going forward (full text at [§6.4](../../research/biryukov-biography-editions/index.md#64-implications-for-citation-practice-in-the-projects-english-version)):

1. **Source-of-record statement** on every chapter or section header: which az.lib.ru file the translation was made from.
2. **Censorship cross-check footnote** on any passage where the Свободное слово, PSS, or Berlin 1921 reading is known to differ.
3. **Validity cross-check footnote** on any passage where the Swedish or Cassell 1911 English supplements differ materially.
4. **Honesty footnote** on any passage where Birukoff's text shows signs of self-censorship the project cannot positively settle. Mark as such; do not fill from secondary sources.

Vol III and Vol IV's existing ledgers do not yet apply this discipline systematically; that is a TODO §8 / §9 follow-up.
